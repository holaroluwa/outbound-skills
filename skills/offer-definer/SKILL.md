# Offer Definer

A weak offer can't be rescued by good copy. This skill forces the offer into the shape that converts: a single clear promise, backed by proof, de-risked, with one obvious next step.

## Input needed
- What you're selling and roughly how it's priced.
- Ideally the value props and pains from `outreach-brief.md` (read them first if present).

## Method
Build the offer in five parts. Each must be specific and true:
1. **One sentence**, what they get, for whom, in plain words.
2. **Outcome**, the concrete result, with a number or timeframe if you have one.
3. **Proof**, why they should believe it: a result, a name, a guarantee, a stat. No proof means a weak offer.
4. **Risk reversal**, what removes the fear of saying yes: a trial, a guarantee, a pilot, no lock-in.
5. **CTA**, the single, low-friction next step. One only.

Pressure-test it: would a skeptical buyer believe each line? If not, fix it or flag the missing proof rather than papering over it.

## Output
Write into **section 2 (Offer)** of `outreach-brief.md`, the five parts clearly labelled. This becomes the spine every copywriting skill points to.
