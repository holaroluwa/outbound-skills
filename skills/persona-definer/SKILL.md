# Persona Definer

You can have the right company and still lose because you wrote to a job title instead of a human under pressure. This skill builds personas you can actually write to.

## Input needed
- The buyer titles you sell to.
- Call notes, transcripts, or context on what their day and pressures look like.

## Method
For each distinct buyer (keep it to the 1-3 that matter), capture:
1. **Title & role**, and where they sit in the decision: user, champion, economic buyer, or blocker.
2. **Day-to-day**, what they're actually responsible for and judged on.
3. **Pressures**, what keeps them up, what their boss leans on them about, what makes them look good or bad.
4. **Their language**, the exact words they use for the problem, pulled verbatim from call notes. This is the gold for copy.
5. **Tone to use back**, how to sound credible to this specific person: peer, expert, or helpful operator.

## Output
Write into **section 4 (Personas)** of `outreach-brief.md`, one block per persona. Make the "their language" lines verbatim wherever you can, because the copywriting skills will lift them directly.
