# Copywriting: LinkedIn DM

LinkedIn is a conversation, not an inbox. The instant you pitch on connect, you're dead. This skill writes messages that open a real exchange.

## Input needed
- The prospect's profile: role, recent activity, anything specific.
- How you found them (the context that makes reaching out make sense).
- Persona, pains, and offer from `outreach-brief.md` if present.

## Method
Write, with two variants each:
1. **Connection request**, under 200 characters, a real reason tied to them, no pitch.
2. **First DM** (after they accept), warm, specific, still no pitch. Start a conversation or offer something useful.
3. **Second DM**, only now a soft bridge to the pain and a low-friction ask.

Pull the prospect's own language and a real signal. Generic equals ignored.

## Voice rules (non-negotiable)
- No em-dashes. No "I'd love to connect and explore synergies."
- Don't pitch on the connection request. Don't pitch in the first DM.
- Short, human, specific. Sound like a peer, not a vendor.

## Output
Connection request (2 variants), first DM (2 variants), and second DM (2 variants), each labelled, with a one-line note on timing between them.
