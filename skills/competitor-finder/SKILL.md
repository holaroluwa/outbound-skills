# Competitor Finder

Deals are won and lost against alternatives, including "do nothing." This skill maps the real competitive field and arms you with questions and handling, not trash-talk.

## Input needed
- Names of competitors and alternatives, including spreadsheets, in-house builds, and the status quo.
- Deal notes where competitors came up, won or lost.

## Method
For each alternative:
1. **What it's genuinely good at**, be honest; pretending it's weak gets you caught.
2. **Where it falls short** for your ICP specifically.
3. **A landmine question**, a fair question you can plant that makes the prospect notice that gap themselves. That lands harder than you attacking it.
4. **Handling script**, a short, calm response for when a prospect says "we're looking at [X]" or "we already use [X]."

Always include **do nothing / status quo** as a competitor, because it's the one you lose to most.

## Output
Write into **section 7 (Competitive landscape)** of `outreach-brief.md`: a block per alternative with the four parts above.
