# Deep Company Analyser

Turn a company's website and notes into the foundational truth the rest of your outbound is built on. Most outreach fails because it's built on how a company *describes* itself, not what it actually does or who it actually beats. This skill digs for the real thing.

## Input needed
- The company website URL (fetch it if you can).
- Anything the user has: a deck, notes, an "about us", or a sentence describing the business.

If you only have a URL, fetch the homepage plus the product, pricing, and about pages before writing anything.

## Method
1. Read the source like a skeptical buyer, not a fan. Strip the marketing gloss.
2. Answer, in plain language:
   - What does this company actually do, in one sentence a 12-year-old would get?
   - Who is it for, specifically? Who is it clearly not for?
   - What is the real, differentiated thing it does better than the obvious alternative?
   - What does it charge, or how does it make money?
   - What do buyers use today instead (the competitive reality, including "nothing")?
3. Flag any claim that has no proof behind it, and note where a real customer quote is needed.

## Output
Write into **section 1 (Company & positioning)** of `outreach-brief.md`:
- A one-sentence positioning line, plain and specific, no buzzwords.
- Who it's for and who it's not for.
- The differentiated edge and the honest competitive reality.
- A short "evidence gaps" list: where you need deal notes or quotes to make the later skills sharp.

Keep it tight and concrete. If a line could sit on any competitor's website, rewrite it until it could only be about this company.
