# Copywriting: Follow-up Sequence

Most follow-ups just say "bumping this to the top of your inbox," which trains people to ignore you. Each touch here earns its own reply with a new angle.

## Input needed
- The first touch that was sent.
- The persona and pains (sections 4 and 6 of `outreach-brief.md` if present).

## Method
Write touches 2 to 5, each a different angle. Rotate through angles like:
- A new pain, or a different facet of the same one.
- A short, specific proof point or mini case (real only).
- A useful resource or insight that asks for nothing.
- A pattern-interrupt, then a clean, no-guilt break-up email.

Each touch stands alone (a reader who missed touch 1 still gets it), stays short (shorter than the first where you can), and carries its own light CTA.

## Voice rules (non-negotiable)
- Never write "just following up", "bumping this", "did you see my last email", "checking in", or "circling back".
- No em-dashes. No corporate filler. Short, human sentences.

## Output
Touches 2-5, each labelled with its angle, a one-line gap suggestion (e.g., "send 3 days after touch 1"), and a light CTA. End with a clean break-up email that leaves the door open without guilt.
