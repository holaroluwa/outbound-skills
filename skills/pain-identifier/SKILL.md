# Pain Identifier

People don't buy features, they buy their way out of pain. This skill finds the real pains in the words buyers actually use, and ranks them so you lead with the one that bites hardest.

## Input needed
- Won-deal call notes or transcripts, where the buyer described their problem.
- Notes from prior outreach: what got replies and what got ignored.

If evidence is thin, say so and draw on the personas in `outreach-brief.md`, but mark guessed pains as unverified.

## Method
1. Pull every moment a buyer described a problem, a frustration, a workaround, or a cost. Capture their exact words.
2. Cluster into distinct pains and merge duplicates.
3. Rank by **urgency** (how much it hurts, how soon) and **frequency** (how often it comes up). The top of the list is what you hook on.
4. Keep one verbatim line of buyer language per pain.

## Output
Write into **section 6 (Top pains)** of `outreach-brief.md`: the top 5 pains, ranked, each with the buyer's own words and a note on urgency and frequency. Lead-gen copy should open on pain #1 unless you have a sharper signal.
